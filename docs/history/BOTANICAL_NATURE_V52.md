# Botanical Nature v52

This build starts from the verified Woodland Nature v51 implementation.

* Woodland layouts, weather settings, menus, and rendering geometry are retained.
* Botanical Nature has separate clock, weather, calendar, and weather-icon assets.
* Botanical weather saves to its own Designer layout file, so it cannot change Woodland edits.
* The Designer selects Botanical images and icon previews when the Botanical weather widget is edited.
