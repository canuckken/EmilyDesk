EMILYDESK BUILD 1348
====================

This build applies the approved proportional clock-hand treatment to the
Industrial theme.

At 100% scale, the approximate visible artwork widths are:

- Hour hand:   11.0 pixels
- Minute hand: 9.4 pixels
- Second hand: 3.7 pixels

The ivory hour hand is modestly wider, the ivory minute hand is close to the
previous hour-hand width, and the amber second hand is now clearly visible
while remaining the finest hand.

Hand lengths, centre pivots, dial geometry, all ten supplied Designer layouts,
personal settings, and every non-Industrial theme remain unchanged.
