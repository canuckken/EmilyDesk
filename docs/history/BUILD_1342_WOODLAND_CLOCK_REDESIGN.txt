EMILYDESK BUILD 1342 — BOTANICAL AND WOODLAND REFINEMENT
========================================================

This build installs the approved Botanical Nature backgrounds and updates the
Woodland Nature clock to match its approved mockup.

- Refined Botanical Clock, Weather, and Calendar artwork with ivory paper,
  sage-green framing, and soft-pink flowers.

- Larger cream Georgia numerals.
- A complete 60-marker dial with stronger hour markers.
- Separate transparent ivory-and-bronze PNG hour and minute hands.
- A slim copper PNG second hand with no tail past the centre.
- All hands retain live local-time movement and a shared centre pivot.
- The existing wood frame and dark-green face remain unchanged.
- Every other theme remains unchanged.

Run Build.cmd on Windows, then install Output\EmilyDeskInstaller.exe.
