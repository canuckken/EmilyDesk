EMILYDESK BUILD 1345
====================

This build includes the supplied DesignerLayouts(3).zip revisions as the new
shipped layout defaults.

Botanical Nature clock changes:

1. The second hand now uses the same forest-green enamel and antique-brass
   colors as the hour and minute hands.
2. The second hand reaches the seconds markers without touching the bezel.
3. The minute hand is one logical desktop pixel wider at 100% scale.

Layout packaging:

- Eight built-in layout files are bundled under Assets\DesignerLayouts.
- The two Ember Glow layouts are bundled inside ThemePackages\EmberGlow.
- Machine-specific paths in the supplied Woodland clock and Steampunk weather
  files were removed from the shipped copies. Their standard bundled artwork
  is selected automatically on every computer.
- The Woodland clock is now included in bundled-layout verification.

Existing personal layouts, assets, weather settings, widget positions, and
other user settings remain untouched and continue to take precedence during
an upgrade.
