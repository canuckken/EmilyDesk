EMILYDESK BUILD 1341 — EMBER GLOW SECOND-HAND FIX
=================================================

This build corrects the remaining Ember Glow second-hand geometry.

- The second hand no longer extends behind or past the centre cap.
- Its forward section is longer and reaches the seconds markers without
  touching the glowing rim.
- Its image pivot remains aligned with the hour hand, minute hand, and centre
  cap at 180,180.
- Ember Glow 1.2.2 is included automatically in fresh installs and upgrades.
- All other widgets, themes, layouts, and settings remain unchanged.

Run Build.cmd on Windows, then install Output\EmilyDeskInstaller.exe.
