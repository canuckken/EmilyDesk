EmilyDesk Build 1371
====================

The Art Deco Calculator is now 300 x 420 design pixels instead of 300 x 390.
Its width and approved Art Deco frame style remain unchanged. The additional
height places the lower frame rail and fan below the bottom button row.

Art Deco Calculator package version: 1.0.4

Expected Windows build output:
  Output\OptionalWidgets\ArtDecoCalculator-1.0.4.emilywidget

Importing version 1.0.4 replaces the earlier Art Deco Calculator package.
The Art Deco Currency Converter and System Information packages are unchanged.
