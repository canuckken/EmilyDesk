EMILYDESK BUILD 1347
====================

This build replaces the barely perceptible width adjustments from Build 1346
with a proportionally balanced Botanical Nature clock-hand set.

At 100% scale, the approximate visible artwork widths are:

- Hour hand:   9.4 pixels
- Minute hand: 8.2 pixels
- Second hand: 3.7 pixels

The hour hand is modestly wider, the minute hand is approximately the width of
the previous hour hand, and the second hand is clearly visible while remaining
the finest hand. The rounded forest-green and antique-brass styling remains
delicate rather than heavy.

Lengths, centre pivots, marker alignment, bezel clearance, the ten supplied
Designer layouts, personal settings, and every other theme remain unchanged.
